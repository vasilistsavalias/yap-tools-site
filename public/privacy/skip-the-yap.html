Skip the Yap!

Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Data handled

Skip the Yap handles provider API keys and preferences, audio from only the voice note you choose, and the transcript returned by your configured provider.

How data is used

  • Keys and preferences are stored in Chrome's local extension storage.
  • When you click Transcribe, the selected audio is sent over HTTPS directly to your configured provider.
  • If you explicitly enable fallback, a retryable failure may send that same selected audio to the next provider you configured.
  • The developer operates no backend and cannot access keys, audio, transcripts, conversations, or settings.

The extension has no analytics, advertising, behavioral tracking, profiling, or transcript history. It does not sell data or use it for credit decisions or unrelated purposes.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Skip the Yap uses selected audio and returned text only for its disclosed transcription purpose and does not permit unrelated human access to it.

Retention and deletion

The extension does not create a transcript history. Use Clear saved keys and settings to remove extension storage, or uninstall the extension to remove its local data.

Provider processing

Provider processing is governed by the terms and privacy policy for your own API use. Use dedicated restricted or spend-limited keys where available.

Changes

Material changes to these data practices will be disclosed in the extension and this policy before release.

Skip the Yap is independent and is not affiliated with Meta or any supported provider.
